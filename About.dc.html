About

The Wildlife Technology Fund builds the missing infrastructure between basic wildlife science and interventions that change animals’ lives at scale — and it runs the programs that get those interventions built.

What we do

Wildlife Technology designs programs to close the gap between basic science and animal interventions. We use developing tools from the innovation landscape — ARPA-style coordinated research programs, milestone payments, focused research organizations — and apply them to the problems that will do the most to solve issues facing and caused by wildlife.

To do this, we focus on developing talent, scoping new program areas, and running coordinated, time-bound programs focused on vetted issues. Two questions shape every program we scope.

Translation

Plenty of promising interventions already exist on paper. Translation means carrying one through the unglamorous middle: manufacturing, field trials, regulatory pathways and cost curves.

Example: a fertility-control bait validated in the lab still needs a delivery system a city can actually afford to deploy.

Bottlenecks

Some fields have been stuck for decades on a single missing tool, dataset or measurement standard. We look for those chokepoints and fund the specific thing that unblocks everyone downstream.

Example: wild welfare assessment stalls without indicators that work across whole taxa and survive field conditions.

Advisors

A program of Renaissance Philanthropy

The Wildlife Technology Fund is a program of Renaissance Philanthropy, which builds and houses funds that push new science and technology toward public benefit. Renaissance Philanthropy provides the operating home for the Fund: fiscal sponsorship, grantmaking infrastructure, legal and finance support, and a network of program designers who have run ARPA-style programs inside and outside government.

That relationship lets a small team act like a much larger one. Program direction, scoping and technical strategy sit with the Fund; the machinery of moving money, contracting researchers and holding programs accountable to milestones sits with Renaissance Philanthropy. Donors give to the Fund through Renaissance Philanthropy, and every program is run to the same standards of review as its sibling funds.

The Fund is primarily supported by the EA Animal Welfare Fund, and works alongside other Renaissance Philanthropy programs where wildlife, AI and metascience overlap.
